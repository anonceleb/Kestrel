Attribute profile #1 — postal fulfilment, DIGIPIN-shaped

An ONDC-logistics-style flow: seller app → LSP, token as an optional payload.

Pilot design, not a protocol rewrite: the capability rides as an optional field inside an existing Beckn action/on_action pair, with the plaintext-address path visibly preserved as a fallback. Nothing here requires every participant to upgrade on day one.

The hop-by-hop flow

Seller app
on_select carries a delivery capability alongside the normal fulfilment block.
plaintext fallback: still present
LSP (DoP-as-LSP)
Receives the grant via confirm. Resolves it exactly once, at depot time — the single online event in this pipeline.
DIGIPIN adapter
Buckets and routes off a truncated 6-of-10-char DIGIPIN prefix. Full DIGIPIN never leaves the vault call.
Courier
Scans an offline-verifiable COSE label — no network, no vault reference, zero address-shaped claims.

RTO unit economics — conservative case

InputValueBasis
RTO rate25–30%lower end of independently cross-checked 20–35% range
Address-error share of RTOs~15%conservative case — headline claims run 18–24%; this page books the lower figure
Per-RTO logistics cost₹180–240logistics-only, conservative against ~₹800 total-loss estimates
Marginal cost of a grant resolution< ₹1one vault call, no new infrastructure per parcel
Addressable saving ≈ ₹7.5 per parcel (25% × 15% × ₹200) against <₹1 marginal cost — the sign of the trade is not close, at the conservative case.
This is the pilot's booked case, not the headline case — see the FIDE convergence memo for the wind-tunnel and both personas' agreed measurement plan. What this page does not claim: that depot-time resolution survives DoP's deep-rural operating reality unmodified — that is a live, unresolved empirical question (D-2 in the same memo), not a settled one. If outage-driven failures eat the savings, the design moves to asynchronous pre-resolution before wider rollout.
